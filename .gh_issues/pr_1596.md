## Description

This PR implements an Employee Share Purchase Plan (ESPP) module with Section 423 Lookback pricing rules and statutory safe-harbor discount calculation.

* **ESPP Enrollment Model** (esppEnrollment.model.js): Tracks employee enrollment periods, contribution limits (1-15%), and accumulated payroll funds.
* **ESPP Transaction Model** (esppTransaction.model.js): Records share purchases, lookback valuation, residual roll-over funds, and taxable perquisite values.
* **ESPP Calculator Service** (esppCalculator.service.js): Implements lookback math min(P_grant, P_purchase) * 0.85, integer share distribution, and batch execution logic.
* **ESPP Controller & Routes** (espp.controller.js, espp.routes.js): Exposes /api/espp/enroll, /api/espp/preview, /api/espp/purchase-run, and /api/espp/transactions.
* **Tests & Documentation**: Added comprehensive unit test coverage and architecture docs.
